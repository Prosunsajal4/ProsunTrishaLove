My Dearest Baby Trisha,

Happy anniversary, my beautiful girl. 💕 I know I say it often, but you really are the best thing that has ever happened to me.

I built this whole website — every pixel, every animation, every silly little sound — because a plain text message never felt like enough for you. You deserve something this big.

I still remember 19th November 2024, our very first conversation. I had no idea that the girl on the other side of the screen was about to become the answer to every prayer I never said out loud. Then, in early 2025, I finally gathered the courage to tell you how I felt — and honestly, my hands were shaking the whole time. What followed were four or five months of quietly waiting for your answer: patient, hopeful, and secretly rehearsing what I would say if you said yes. You were worth every single one of those days.

Then came 9th July 2025 — the day you finally said "YES" and we made it official. The world literally looked brighter that morning. And the very next day, 10th July 2025, I got to see you in person for the first time. You were even more beautiful than I had imagined, and I forgot every single word I had planned to say.

Thank you for being my best friend, my biggest fan, and the calmest place I know. You are my brilliant Miss Engineer, and I will always be your proud Mr. Engineer. Whether we are debugging code at 2 AM, arguing about nothing, or laughing until tears stream down our faces — life just makes more sense with you in it.

Every line of code on this site is only me trying to say the same thing a thousand different ways: I love you, Trisha. Today, tomorrow, and forever.

I promise to choose you — on the easy days and on the hard ones. To listen, to hold your hand, to keep making you laugh until we are old and grey. Here is to everything behind us, and to a whole lifetime of us still ahead. 🥂

With all my soul & forever yours,

Your Prosun ❤️
